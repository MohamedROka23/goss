FULL DESCRIPTION (EN)
--------------------------------------------------
GOSST is a one-stop business partner for companies and organizations in Egypt, connecting them to reliable supplies, trade, and logistics services — built specifically for the HORECA sector (hotels, restaurants, catering) and corporate customers.

Why GOSST?
• Request quotes in seconds. Browse the catalog, save items to your cart, and send a request directly to our management team.
• Live price quotes. Our team reviews every request and replies with exact prices and tailored offers.
• Supplies & Trade. Food, consumables, and general supplies sourced through verified channels.
• Logistics & Transport. Freight, distribution, and delivery services coordinated by our operations team.
• Everything in one place. Your requests, quotes, and history stay in sync.
• Instant notifications. Be notified the moment a quote is ready or an order status changes.

How it works:
1. Explore the catalog or request a custom quote.
2. Fill in the request form with your details.
3. Send it — our team follows up with the offer.
4. Confirm, and we arrange delivery to your location.

GOSST is operated in Alexandria, Egypt.
For support, contact us at Info@gossts.com.
